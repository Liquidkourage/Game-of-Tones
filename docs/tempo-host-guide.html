1 · How a TEMPO night works

TEMPO is music bingo. Instead of numbers, every square on a player's card is a song. You play short clips from Spotify; players who recognize a song find it on their card (on their phone) and tap it. First verified pattern wins the round.

The three screens

ScreenWho sees itWhat it does
Host viewYou onlyBuild rounds, control playback, verify bingos. Tabs: Game Rounds Players Display Settings
Public displayProjector / TVSplash screen with QR join code before play; the live call list during play (song titles reveal gradually)
Player viewEach player's phoneTheir bingo card; they tap squares as songs play and call BINGO from there

Nights, rounds, and playlists

A night is made of rounds (plan up to 12). Each round uses exactly 1 or exactly 5 playlists — nothing in between:

  • 1 playlist (Round Mix): all 75 card songs come from one big playlist. Simple, classic.
  • 5 playlists (Columns): each playlist owns one card column. Players know which theme each column is — a great hook for themed rounds.

Option — your own letters

The column letters don't have to be B-I-N-G-O. In Settings → Defaults → Column letters, set any five letters (T-E-M-P-O, T-O-N-E-S…). They appear on cards, the projector, and printed cards.

Key concept — one shuffle per round

When you press Start game, TEMPO shuffles the round's 75 songs into one play order (#1–75). The host list, the projector call numbers, and playback all follow that same order for the whole round.

2 · One-time setup & your defaults

  1. Sign in with your approved host account and create a room. Your room code is what players and the projector connect to.
  2. Connect Spotify from the Connection panel (in the header, or Settings → Playback & connections). You need Spotify Premium. Music plays on whichever Spotify device you pick — the venue PC, a tablet, your phone.
  3. Pick a playback device. Open Spotify on the device you want, then choose it in the Connection panel. An idle device is fine — TEMPO transfers playback to it automatically at Start game. Only offline devices need attention (tap Refresh devices after waking it).
  4. Set your defaults in Settings. These save to your account, so they follow you to every event and device.

3 · Building rounds — the Rounds tab

This is your planning workspace. Do it before doors open — it autosaves to your account as you go.

  1. Browse the Playlist Library. You'll see the shared Tempo library plus your own Spotify playlists. Use the picks/All toggle to filter to curated lists.
  2. Assign playlists to rounds. Three ways:
    • Drag a playlist onto a round chip in "Tonight's rounds"
    • Double-click a playlist to add it to the currently focused round
    • Use the playlist's assign menu to pick specific rounds
  3. Respect the 1-or-5 rule. A round must end at exactly 1 or exactly 5 playlists. With 5, the order you set = the card column order, left to right.
  4. Set each round's card rules (you can also do this later in Step 2 of the cockpit): win pattern, lines required, free space.
  5. Save your rounds. Save all and return to Game snapshots every eligible round so cards and pools are ready to deal instantly at showtime.

Options while planning

  • Reusing a playlist in two rounds is allowed — you'll see a caution badge so it's a deliberate choice.
  • Leftovers is a virtual playlist of every song that hasn't been played yet tonight — a fun closer round.
  • Printables: per round you can download printed card PDFs (pick how many, watermark-preview first) and a host call sheet. Useful for paper-card venues or backup.
  • Add / remove rounds any time before they're played (up to 12).

Step 3 · Play game — the readiness check

A pre-show checklist confirms, live:

  • Playback connected (Spotify linked)
  • Spotify device ready — "idle" is OK; it activates at Start game
  • Projector display connected — open /display/YOURROOM on the venue screen if not
  • Bingo pool built — use Build song pool if you didn't save the round earlier
  • Round structure valid (1 or 5 playlists)

Activate the event

If your org uses event credits, activate the event from Step 3 (or Settings) before starting Round 1. Trials and unlimited plans just confirm.

Meanwhile, on the projector…

The splash screen shows the room QR code and join URL. Players scan, enter a name, and wait — they get cards when you set the round.

5 · Setting and starting a round

  1. Press Set round (Step 3, once the round is ready). This deals player cards and replaces the splash with the call list — five columns with this round's playlist names as headers. Nothing is playing yet.
  2. Break down the round for the crowd. This is your mic moment: explain the themes, the win pattern, how many lines, free space, how title reveals work. Players are looking at their fresh cards while you talk.
  3. Need the QR back? Back to splash returns the join screen — late arrivals can still scan. Set round again when you're ready.
  4. Press Start game. TEMPO shuffles the play order, transfers playback to your Spotify device, and clip #1 begins. From here the live dashboard takes over.

6 · Running live play

During a round the Game tab becomes a live dashboard: current track, round progress (played / remaining), player count, and playback controls.

ControlWhat it does
Pause / ResumeStops and restarts the clip clock and Spotify playback
Replay clipPlays the current song's clip again
Mark played / skipLogs the current song and moves to the next in order

What players see: each clip, they hunt their card and tap matches. What the projector shows: each played song joins the call list with its call number; titles start masked and reveal per your reveal mode, so latecomers can't just read answers.

Good habits

  • Stay on whatever tab you like — playback won't yank you back to Game.
  • You can keep prepping later rounds on the Rounds tab mid-game; browsing rounds never moves the live round.
  • If a song title is wrong or has multiple valid titles, you can edit titles/aliases from the host pool list.

7 · Bingo calls & verification

  1. A player taps BINGO on their phone. Playback pauses automatically and a verification alert appears for you (claims queue first-in, first-out if several land at once).
  2. Open verification. You'll see the player's full card with their marked squares, validated against the round's pattern and the songs actually played.
  3. Approve if it's legit — the round ends, the winner celebration shows, and the projector announces it. Reject if not (wrong squares, pattern not met) — play resumes where you left off.

After an approved bingo — your options

  • Back to host — close the celebration, decide later (round is marked complete)
  • Prepare next round (no auto-start) — loads the next planned round for review; start it when ready (recommended)
  • Start next planned round now — jumps straight in
  • Start next round (fresh) — resets to a blank setup if you want to improvise a round

8 · Between rounds & ending the night

The multi-round loop

For each later round, the rhythm repeats:

Prepare next round → review Step 3 checklist → Set round (new cards + new headers) → explain → Start game

Players keep their connection between rounds — they get a fresh card automatically each time you set a round. The projector flips back to the new call list.

Event actions (bottom of the Game tab)

ActionWhen to use it
End roundNo bingo and the pool's run dry, or time's up — stops playback, clears cards, marks the round complete
Reset roundFalse start — stops playback and clears cards but keeps the same round active to start again
Next plannedSkip ahead to the next planned round
Reset eventSet every round back to unplanned (saved snapshots keep their playlists)
Clear prep cacheWipe saved round prep for this room in this browser

Ending the night

  • Export a recap (Settings → Recap → Export JSON): rounds, songs played, winners — handy for prize records.
  • Close the event from the activation bar when your org tracks credits.
  • Sign out or just close — your preferences and saved prep live in your account for next time.

Troubleshooting

SymptomFix
Device shows "idle"That's fine — Start game activates it. Or use the Activate device button in Connection.
Device missing from listOpen Spotify on it, press play/pause once, tap Refresh devices.
Spotify "having trouble" while loading playlistsUsually a temporary Spotify hiccup — TEMPO retries and falls back to cached tracks. Wait a moment and rebuild the pool.
Projector looks stale after changesHard-refresh the display browser tab (Ctrl+Shift+R).
Round won't save / startCheck the playlist count — exactly 1 or exactly 5, nothing else.
