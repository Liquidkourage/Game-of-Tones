What Counts As An Event?

One event credit covers one activated event night — up to twelve rounds in the same room, whether you run a single long game or a full multi-round residency-style lineup.

  • Activate when you are ready to print room-ready materials or go live.
  • Running two venues the same evening? Each room uses its own credit — simple and fair.
  • Plan ahead for free: round buckets, patterns, playlists, and a one-card preview PDF.

Fair, flexible, venue-ready

Built for how you actually run shows.

TEMPO pricing is designed to reward success in the room — not punish it. Here is how credits, rollover, and refunds work in plain language.

Included With Every Event

  • Up to twelve rounds in one activated night (same room).
  • Full printable PDFs — bingo cards and host call sheets.
  • Live digital play with unlimited players on their phones.
  • Projector / public display with professional call flow.
  • Round planner: patterns, free space, snippets, saved prep.
  • Multiple simultaneous events across rooms — each uses its own credit.

Free Before You Activate

  • Build round buckets and drag in playlists.
  • Set bingo patterns and host preferences.
  • Save prep locally in your browser.
  • Preview layout with one watermarked sample card per round.
  • No credit used until you print the full deck or start the live game.

Credits & Rollover — Quick Reference

Topic How it works
Consumption order Monthly plan credits first → rollover credits (oldest first) → pack inventory → block with upgrade options.
Rollover Up to 3 unused monthly events roll forward. They expire after 1 month and apply only while your subscription is active.
Event window An activated event stays open for up to 36 hours or until you end it — enough time for afternoon prep and evening showtime.
Accidental activation If a show never really started (≤3 songs, no players, no full PDF printed), the credit can auto-refund. Printed materials mean the night was delivered — no refund.
Enterprise Unlimited activations. Same workflow and reporting — no per-event billing.
